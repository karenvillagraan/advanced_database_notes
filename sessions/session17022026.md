## What I understood
- I learned that joins in SQL allow you to retrieve related data that is stored across multiple tables. This reflects the main principle of relational databases, where information is separated into different tables to keep the database organized and avoid redundancy. Instead of storing all data in one large and inefficient table, joins make it possible to dynamically connect the relevant records when the information is needed.
